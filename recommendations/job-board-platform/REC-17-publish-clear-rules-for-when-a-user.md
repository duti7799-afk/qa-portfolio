# REC-17: Publish clear rules for when a user can be blocked

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Job Board & Community Platform |
| **Priority** | High |
| **Status** | Accepted |

## Current Behavior
Admins can block any user, but there are no published rules explaining what leads to a block.

## Problem
Users are not informed in advance what is allowed and what the consequences of violations are.

## Proposed Solution
Add terms and rules describing which violations lead to a temporary or permanent block.

## Justification
Increases transparency, trust and safety.
