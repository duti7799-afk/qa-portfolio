# REC-05: Configure operator working hours per operator, not globally

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | AI Shopping Assistant |
| **Priority** | High |
| **Status** | Submitted |

## Current Behavior
Changing the working hours of one operator updates the schedule of all operators.

## Problem
Individual shifts cannot be managed and the system shows wrong availability for the team, which hurts customer support.

## Proposed Solution
Store working-hours settings at the individual operator level.

## Justification
Removes data conflicts between operator profiles and makes shift management accurate.
