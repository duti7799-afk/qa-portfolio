# REC-20: Add onboarding hints for swipe right / left

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Dating Mobile App |
| **Priority** | — |
| **Status** | In review |

## Current Behavior
There is no visual hint explaining what swiping right or left means.

## Problem
New users may not understand the main interaction.

## Proposed Solution
On first open, show an overlay with an animated hand and text (“Swipe right to like”, “Swipe left to skip”). While swiping, gradually show a green “LIKE” stamp or heart.

## Justification
Clearer and easier first-time experience.
