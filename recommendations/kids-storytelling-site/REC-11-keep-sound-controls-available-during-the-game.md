# REC-11: Keep sound controls available during the game

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Kids' Interactive Storytelling Website |
| **Priority** | Low |
| **Status** | Submitted |

## Current Behavior
Sound and music can be adjusted or muted only while listening to the story; the controls disappear during the game.

## Problem
Users cannot control sound while playing.

## Proposed Solution
Show the same controls during the game — either two mute/unmute icons, or the same volume controls as in story mode.

## Justification
Better UI/UX and easier use of the product.
