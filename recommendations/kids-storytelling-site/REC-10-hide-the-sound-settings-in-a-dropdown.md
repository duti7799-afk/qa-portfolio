# REC-10: Hide the sound settings in a dropdown behind a settings icon

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Kids' Interactive Storytelling Website |
| **Priority** | — |
| **Status** | Submitted |

## Current Behavior
During the game, the sound and music sliders are fixed in the top-centre of the screen and cover the main illustration.

## Problem
The panel looks untidy and hides part of the picture.

## Proposed Solution
Show only a settings icon in a corner; on click, open the sliders as a dropdown or popup, and hide them again on a second click or a click elsewhere.

## Justification
Cleaner, minimal UI and a better visual experience.
