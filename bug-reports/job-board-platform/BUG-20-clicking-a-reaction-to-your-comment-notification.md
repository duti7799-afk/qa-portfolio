# BUG-20: Clicking a “reaction to your comment” notification opens the home page instead of the related post

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Job Board & Community Platform |
| **Priority** | Normal |
| **Environment** | Staging · Lenovo ThinkPad · Windows 11 Pro · Google Chrome 147 |
| **Reproducibility** | 100% |

## Preconditions
User is logged in, is on any page except the home page, has commented on another user’s blog or job post, and somebody reacted to that comment.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the notifications panel.
2. Click the reaction notification.

## Expected Result
The user is taken to the post where the comment was left, so they can see who reacted.

## Actual Result
The user is taken to the home page.
